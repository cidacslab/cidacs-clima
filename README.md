The **CIDACS Climate and Environmental Platform (CIDACS-Clima)** is a data resource developed by the **Center for Data and Knowledge Integration for Health (CIDACS), Oswaldo Cruz Foundation (Fiocruz)**, to support research on the relationships between climate, environmental exposures, and health in Brazil.

CIDACS-Clima integrates climate and environmental data from multiple sources and provides data, metadata, computational resources, and tools to support epidemiological, environmental, and public health research.

## Objectives

The platform aims to:

- integrate climate and environmental data relevant to health research;
- support the analysis of climate and environmental exposures at different spatial and temporal scales;
- facilitate the integration of environmental information with health and population data;
- provide reproducible data-processing workflows and computational resources;
- support research on climate change, environmental determinants of health, and health inequalities in Brazil.

## Repository contents

This repository may contain:

- data-processing and integration workflows;
- scripts and computational tools;
- metadata and data dictionaries;
- documentation;
- examples and supporting resources;
- configuration files and reproducibility materials.
